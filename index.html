We make air filters. You set the size.

Sors HVAC is an air filter manufacturer in Byala, Varna. We build filters for ventilation units, air handling units, workshops, kitchens and homes, in standard sizes and in any size you measure.

Every order comes down to three choices: the model, the filter class and the frame. The rest is your measurements.

Models
Panel, round, cartridge, pocket and V-bank, or a shape of your own.
Filter classes
G4, M5, F7, F9 and H13 (HEPA).
Sizes
From 5 cm to 150 cm, set to the millimetre.
Frames
Steel, aluminium, plastic or cardboard, in eight colours or one you choose.
Quantity
One filter or hundreds, with discounts from five pieces.

From your measurements to a finished filter

  1. 1

    Measure

    You enter the size, class and frame online.

  2. 2

    Cut

    We cut the filter media to your dimensions.

  3. 3

    Pleat

    The media is folded into pleats, which give a large surface in a small frame.

  4. 4

    Frame

    Frame and seal are fitted so no air passes around the media.

  5. 5

    Check and ship

    Each filter is checked and packed for delivery.
